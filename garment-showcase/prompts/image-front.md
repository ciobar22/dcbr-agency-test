# Front anchor

Attach your own front product photograph. Add a back photograph only as a secondary identity reference. Replace the bracketed values, then use an image-editing tool. This prompt alone does not provide access to one.

```text
Create a photographic studio product image of this exact [garment] hanging on thin wooden shoulders, viewed directly from the front. Preserve its real cut, fabric color, seams, labels and [describe the visible print]. Do not invent new graphics or details.

Use a locked camera, neutral soft studio lighting and a 1280×720 landscape canvas. Show the entire garment with generous clearance around sleeves and hem. Place the top wooden suspension apex at the horizontal center, near the top of the canvas. Keep this apex, garment scale and camera unchanged for the next image.

Use one completely flat bright [green #00ff00 / magenta #ff00ff] background with no gradient, backdrop shadow or colored reflections on the garment. Choose a backing color absent from the garment and its print.

Exclude metal hooks, wires, rails, mannequins, humans, text and extra props. Produce one image.
```

Approve silhouette, print and color against the photograph. Save the accepted result as `inputs/front.png`. Green garments need a different backing, for example magenta. The supplied anchors are neutral illustrations for testing the request shape, not product-production inputs.
