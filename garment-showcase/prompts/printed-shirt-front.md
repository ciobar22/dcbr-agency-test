# From a supplied print to a garment concept

Attach the graphic as an artwork reference. Use graphics you may process and publish.

```text
Photorealistic product photograph of one oversized heavyweight [white / pastel pink] cotton T-shirt, hanging from a dark walnut wooden hanger. Direct front view, fixed chest-height camera, entire garment visible with generous margins. Realistic cotton weave, seams and restrained natural folds. Soft even studio light.

Place the supplied graphic on the center chest. Preserve its complete composition, colors, lines and letters. The print looks like ink on cotton. For black line art, remove the source paper background. No added branding, text, label, person or mannequin.

Uniform saturated chroma-green background with no floor, shadow or green spill. No hook, wire or rail; the interface supplies the fixed suspension wire.
```

Compare the resulting print against the exact source file. If the image model redraws it, generate a blank cotton base and composite the original artwork, carrying the local fabric shading into the ink. This keeps the image anchor faithful; a video model can still alter the print during the turn, so review its intermediate frames separately.

Derive the strict profile using [image-profile.md](image-profile.md). Keep the same canvas, object height and wooden apex. Use the accepted front as the video's last frame.
