Behind the name

Pir means feast.

pir

Slavic root: banquet

pirog

the large filled pie

pirukas

in Estonian – piirakka in Finnish

pidu

every pie is a feast

The pie is a shared tradition across Slavic and Finnic cultures. It gives Pirogoff a story that speaks equally to Estonians, Russian-speaking locals, Finns and tourists.
