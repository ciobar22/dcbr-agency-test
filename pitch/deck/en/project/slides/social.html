Three repeatable formats that build trust.

“POV: a pie is born”

5 am in the Tiskre kitchen: dough, filling, braid, oven. A 20–30 second first-person video.

“Pie on test”

An honest review series: unboxing, the cut, first bite, the score. Customers and local creators.

“Feast pies”

Stories from tables: birthdays, holidays, family. Pir = feast – every pie is a feast.

Rhythm: 3–4 posts a week · a review card in every box · a Google profile for every counter.
